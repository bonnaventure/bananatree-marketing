#### Case Study : Small Business SEO and Digital Marketing

Being a one-person operation offered a lot of freedom and flexibility for Strike Plumbing and Heating LTD. However, without digital marketing support the website, Google Business Page, and SEO had become stale, and they were not even sure who was hosting their website. We partnered with Strike to close the gaps, freshen the content, and put together a plan to drive more business through digital channels.
