#### The Challenge

As a small local business, Strike Plumbing and Heating LTD. was operating without much digital marketing support beyond a basic website. The owner had setup the website with an agency when the business first launched, and it had been serving them relatively well. He felt it could use a refresh, but he wasn't sure how to update the pages or who was hosting the site. 

#### The Work

Working one-on-one with the client, we were able to determine who was responsible for the website and create a working relationship with the vendor. Once established, we worked with the client to refresh the website content with updated photography, product/service descriptions and contact information. Additionally we reworked the Google Business Page listing to better reflect the business's offerings and brand. Finally, we have put together a multi-phase plan that fits the business's budget and business goals, including search engine marketing (PPC/SEM), social media, email/SMS, and eventually a more robust website with a modern Content Management System (CMS).

#### The Impact

Within weeks of the Google Business Page refresh, we began to see an increase in website visits, phone calls, and other positive interactions on the profile. These interactions translated in to more work for Strike, and an increase in revenue. We also had to adjust our plan, as the increase in customer calls made it necessary for the owner to make preparations for additional jobs.

#### Techniques 

1. Google Business Page updates and optimization.
2. SEO content refresh.
3. Website audit and vendor communication.
4. Digital marketing strategy.
